ABOUT

The story behind the shadow. The people behind the legend.

OUR HISTORY

Clash of BaNG is a growing fan community inspired by the world-famous Clash-style fantasy universe. What began as a small online forum in 2018 has quickly expanded into one of the most active strategy communities in Southeast Asia.

Driven by a shared passion for dark fantasy warfare and deep tactical gameplay, the founders built Clash of BaNG from the ground up, starting with nothing more than a Discord server and a dream. Today, the community spans three regional hubs and continues to grow at a rapid pace.

The community provides various content including troop guides, hero strategies, fan art collections, and beginner tutorials, with a special focus on Dark Troops, which remain the most beloved unit class among the founders and veteran members alike.

2018

Clash of BaNG launched as a small online forum. Within 48 hours of going live, over 10,000 players signed up, marking the beginning of the shadow warfare era.

2020

The community expanded beyond the forum, establishing its first regional hub in Jakarta. Troop guides and Dark Troop breakdowns became the most-visited content on the site.

2022

Two new regional hubs opened in Bandung and Sanur, bringing the total to three active community centers. Live tournaments were introduced for the first time.

2024

Clash of BaNG surpassed 5 million active players worldwide. The founders announced plans to launch a fully redesigned official website to serve the global fanbase.
